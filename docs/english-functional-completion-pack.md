## 1. `prepare_smallest_step_short`

**Family** prepare &nbsp;&nbsp; **Technique key** `smallest_viable_action` &nbsp;&nbsp; **Version** 1

**Purpose.** Shrink starting to something too small to refuse.

**When it is useful.** The task is clear but beginning it is not happening.

**How it differs.** nr_prepare_short rehearses the first action in detail. This one makes the first action smaller than deciding.

> What is the smallest version of starting? Not the whole task — the first thirty seconds of it. Opening the file. Writing the subject line. Saying the first sentence out loud to nobody. Pick something that small, and let that be the entire plan for now. The rest can decide itself once you are moving. Thirty seconds of movement is worth more than an hour of intending to.

## 2. `prepare_done_before_short`

**Family** prepare &nbsp;&nbsp; **Technique key** `prior_competence_recall` &nbsp;&nbsp; **Version** 1

**Purpose.** Recall prior competence as evidence, not encouragement.

**When it is useful.** Facing something familiar that still feels daunting.

**How it differs.** The only module that looks backwards for evidence rather than forwards at the task.

> You have done something like this before. Maybe not this exact thing, but something that asked the same of you — and you are here, so it went well enough. That version of you is not a different person. Whatever you used then, you still have. You are not starting from nothing. Give yourself credit for the evidence rather than arguing with it.

## 3. `prepare_what_matters_short`

**Family** prepare &nbsp;&nbsp; **Technique key** `priority_reduction` &nbsp;&nbsp; **Version** 1

**Purpose.** Cut a crowded agenda down to what would actually count.

**When it is useful.** Several things ahead and no sense of which is load-bearing.

**How it differs.** focus_narrow_short narrows attention in the moment; this chooses between competing obligations.

> Of everything ahead, what actually matters? Not what is loudest, or newest, or most recently asked for — what would you be glad you handled. Usually it is one thing, sometimes two. The rest will still happen, or it will quietly turn out not to have mattered. Give the one thing first claim on you. Everything else can queue behind it without complaint. It usually does.

## 4. `prepare_ready_enough_short`

**Family** prepare &nbsp;&nbsp; **Technique key** `readiness_reframe` &nbsp;&nbsp; **Version** 1

**Purpose.** Separate readiness from feeling ready.

**When it is useful.** Waiting for confidence that is not going to arrive first.

**How it differs.** nr_reframe_short reframes nerves as energy. This one reframes the requirement to feel anything.

> You do not have to feel ready. Feeling ready mostly arrives after starting, not before it, which is an awkward order but a reliable one. Waiting for it is how things get postponed indefinitely. You are allowed to begin unconvinced. Start anyway, and let the confidence catch up in its own time. Most people who look ready are simply people who began a few minutes ago.

## 5. `prepare_afterwards_short`

**Family** prepare &nbsp;&nbsp; **Technique key** `completion_imagery` &nbsp;&nbsp; **Version** 1

**Purpose.** Step past the event to the ordinary evening after it.

**When it is useful.** Anticipation has grown larger than the thing itself.

**How it differs.** transition_urgency_fades_short observes urgency decaying in the past. This looks forward to it.

> Think past the thing for a moment. Afterwards — when it is behind you and the day has carried on. It will be an ordinary evening and this will be something that happened earlier. That is the most likely outcome by a wide margin. You are walking toward a version of today where this is already done. You are closer to that evening than you are to the version where it goes wrong.

## 6. `focus_one_question_short`

**Family** focus &nbsp;&nbsp; **Technique key** `single_question_framing` &nbsp;&nbsp; **Version** 1

**Purpose.** Reduce a scattered situation to one answerable question.

**When it is useful.** Several half-formed questions arriving at once.

**How it differs.** focus_narrow_short picks a thing; this picks a QUESTION, which is what scatter is usually made of.

> Reduce it to one question. Not the whole situation — one question you could actually answer in the next few minutes. Scattered attention is usually several questions arriving at once, all half-asked. Pick one. Let it be the only one for now.

## 7. `focus_close_the_rest_short`

**Family** focus &nbsp;&nbsp; **Technique key** `context_closing` &nbsp;&nbsp; **Version** 1

**Purpose.** Close the mental tabs still being half-held.

**When it is useful.** Nothing is urgent but nothing is finished either.

**How it differs.** settle_nothing_to_decide_short defers decisions for the night. This closes open loops mid-day.

> Everything you are half-holding is still open. Not on a screen — in you. The half-finished message, the thing you meant to check, the conversation you have not replied to. You can close them for a while. They will reopen when you need them, and nothing is lost by not holding them all at once.

## 8. `focus_return_is_the_skill_short`

**Family** focus &nbsp;&nbsp; **Technique key** `attentional_return` &nbsp;&nbsp; **Version** 1

**Purpose.** Normalise wandering, and make returning the point.

**When it is useful.** Attention keeps slipping and that is becoming the problem.

**How it differs.** The only module that addresses the experience of concentrating rather than what to concentrate on.

> Your attention will wander. That is not a fault in you and it is not a sign this is going badly. The returning is the part that counts, and you can do it as many times as it takes. Wander, return. Wander, return. That is what concentrating actually looks like from the inside.

## 9. `focus_next_ten_minutes_short`

**Family** focus &nbsp;&nbsp; **Technique key** `horizon_shortening` &nbsp;&nbsp; **Version** 1

**Purpose.** Shorten the horizon to a length that can be seen.

**When it is useful.** The scale of what is ahead is the obstacle.

**How it differs.** prepare_smallest_step_short shrinks the ACTION; this shrinks the TIME.

> Shrink the horizon. Not today, not this week — the next ten minutes. That is a length of time you can actually see the edges of. Ten minutes is enough to start something and not long enough to dread. When it ends you can choose again, with better information than you have now.

## 10. `focus_any_direction_short`

**Family** focus &nbsp;&nbsp; **Technique key** `direction_over_optimisation` &nbsp;&nbsp; **Version** 1

**Purpose.** Prefer a reasonable direction over the best one.

**When it is useful.** Stuck comparing options rather than moving.

**How it differs.** nr_prepare_short assumes the move is known. This is for when it is not.

> A direction does not have to be the right one. Standing still comparing options costs more than picking a reasonable one and finding out. You can correct while moving; you cannot correct while stuck. Choose the one that looks sensible from here and let the next step tell you more.

## What this pack does not fix

`wound_up_home` at 600s, 900s and 1200s stays broken — nine of the sixty
combinations.

It is not a shortage of families. At 1200s the `settle` phase is starved
because `reconnect_to_now` runs immediately before it, is allocated 346
seconds, and accepts the same families — `ground`, `transition`, `settle`. It
chains through the shared pool and leaves nothing behind.

Measured: **fifteen further `ground` or `settle` modules** clear it. Eight do
not. Those fifteen would exist for no reason except to out-supply a greedy
phase — 45 provider generations of content whose purpose is to survive being
eaten by the phase before it.

The allocator is deliberately greedy and legible, and is not being changed
without a product decision. But the choice is worth making deliberately:

- **Author fifteen more modules** — 45 generations, and real depth for novelty
  once it has somewhere to go.
- **Give the allocator lookahead or a reservation rule** — a code change to the
  engine, which reaches 60/60 with no further content.
- **Accept 51/60** — `wound_up_home` offered only at 300s, and the longer
  durations hidden for that one recipe.

Nothing in this pack presumes which.
